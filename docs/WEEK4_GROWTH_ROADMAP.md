## Strategy 2: Social Sharing

**Current State**: Basic share buttons exist
**Goal**: Make sharing irresistible

**Improvements**:
1. **One-click sharing**
   - Pre-filled captions
   - Optimized image previews
   - Track shares for virality scoring

2. **Viral hooks**
   - 'This meme made 1,234 people laugh'
   - 'Share to unlock next meme' (gamification)
   - 'Challenge a friend to a meme battle'

3. **Platform-specific optimization**
   - Reddit: Include 'via memeexplorer.com'
   - Twitter: Optimal image dimensions
   - Instagram Stories: Vertical format

## Strategy 3: Reddit/Twitter Presence

**Reddit Strategy**:
- Post to r/memes, r/funny (with attribution)
- Engage authentically, not spam
- Respond to comments
- Weekly 'best of' posts

**Twitter Bot**:
- Auto-post trending memes every 4 hours
- Include relevant hashtags
- Respond to mentions
- Run polls: 'Which meme is funnier?'
